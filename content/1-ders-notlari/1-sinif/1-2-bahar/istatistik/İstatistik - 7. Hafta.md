### a) Mod
$L_1 = 18$ <br>
$\Delta_1 = 9-1 = 8 \space \space \space \space\space \space \space | \space \space \space\space \space \space \space \Delta_2 = 9-0 = 9$ <br>

$\text{Mod} = 18 + \frac{48}{17} = 20,823$


### b) Medyan
$\frac{n}{2} = \frac{20}{2} = 10 \space \space \space \space \space \space \space | \space \space \space \space \space \space \space L_1 = 6$ <br>
$\Sigma f_1 = 2 \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space | \space \space \space \space \space \space \space f_{\text{med}} = 8$ <br>
$\text{Medyan} = 6 + (\frac{10-2}{8}).6 = 12$ 



### c) $Q_1$
$\frac{n}{4} = \frac{20}{4} = 5 \space \space \space \space \space \space \space | \space \space \space \space  \space \space \space L_1 = 6$ <br>
$\Sigma f_1 = 2   \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space \space | \space \space \space \space \space \space \space f_{Q_1} = 8$ <br>
$Q_1 = 6 + (\frac{5-2}{8}).6$ <br> 
$Q_1 = 6 + \frac{18}{8} = 6 + 2,25 = 8,25$
